# Insights

Stats shows three completion rates, a 12-week heatmap, and the current streak of each active habit.

Today counts habits that are due today. The week follows the week-start setting. The month is the current calendar month, and it stops at today so future days are not failures. A daily or weekday habit is one opportunity per due day. A times-per-week habit is one opportunity per target, capped so extra completions do not raise the rate above 100%.

The heatmap uses the same day rule and skips times-per-week habits, because those are scored by week.

Finishing a habit celebrates the first time its current streak reaches 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, or 100. Clearing the day and finishing it again can celebrate the same milestone a second time, because the streak crossed it again.
